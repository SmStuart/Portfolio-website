Digital Experience Design

Goal

To research, design, and present a UX- driven prototype for a mobile self-care app, demonstrating both theoretical knowledge and practical design skill through a structured well-documented design process.

About

The Art of Eight Lumps is a Muay Thai training and self-care app that supports users throughout every stage of their training. It combines training tracking, recovery management, technique analysis, and mental well-being into a single platform, giving users the tools they need to improve their performance, recover effectively, and stay consistent. Instead of being just another fitness app. it is designed specifically for Muay Thai practitioners to help them train smarter prevent injuries and avoid burnout. 

Purpose

The goal is to provide Muay Thai practitioners with an all-in-one amp that supports every aspect of their training journey. By tracking training, recovery, daily habits, technique progression and mental well-being in a single platform, the app helps users train smarter, recover more effectively, reduce the risk of injury, and stay consistent with their long-term goals. 

Gap

Most fitness apps track general performance but overlook the specific needs of Muay Thai practitioners. This app combines training, recovery, and mental well-being into one platform to help users train smarter and recover better. 

Key Features

Fighter Self‑Care System

Mental Readiness (mood, stress, confidence, focus)

Burnout Detection (flags fatigue patterns).

Sleep + Training Load Integration (recommends rest/light sessions).

Injury‑Risk Prediction (tracks soreness and overuse trends).

Other Features

Workout Tracker (Logs padwork, bagwork, sparring, clinch rounds, conditioning, and strength sessions.)

Daily Habit System (Tracks mobility, shin conditioning, shadowboxing, meditation, and journaling).

Sleep Tracker (Monitors sleep duration and quality for recovery readiness).

Diet and Calorie Tracker (Logs meals, calories, macros, and hydration).

Find a Local Gym (Google Maps integration to locate nearby Muay Thai gyms).

Round Timer (Customisable timer for training rounds.).
