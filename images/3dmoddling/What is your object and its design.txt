What is your object and its design?
The object is a crt television manufactured in the 80/90's. It has reflects the design aesthetic for this design period.

What is their unique property that relates to their world?
The world's setting is an 80/90's dystopian post apocalypse. Where nothing new is made anymore. The inhabitants have to repair and make do with what they have. All the objects in this will are nearly completely worn out or they have been repaired so much that they do not resemble the original look and feel they had previously. The unique properties are consistent for this time period. Due to the limited processing power computers had. Visualising 3d models on a computer screen and making these 3d models into physical objects look and feel a certain way. These are: rounded and bevel plastic, sleek flat surfaces and a distinct boxy look. 

Why did you choose each property of your object?
The properties of the object were chosen because they communicate a particular feeling and look, which is consistent with this time period. I wanted the view to experience this as well.
